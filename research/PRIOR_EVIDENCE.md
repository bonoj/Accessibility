# Prior Evidence and Lineage

Status: research note  
Date: 2026-09-27

Accessibility begins from prior work but does not fork or duplicate that work.

This note records useful antecedents so future collaborators can inspect them at their source.

## World Lab

Source: https://github.com/bonoj/world-lab

World Lab provides the current research practice inherited by this repository:

- executable evidence can earn semantic and architectural promotion;
- a durable Semantic Surface can describe present-tense system truth;
- change records can preserve why consequential boundaries changed;
- research can preserve unanswered questions without granting them authority;
- implementation, semantics, research, and exact Git lineage can remain distinct;
- independent systems can retain behavioral ownership while sharing only the semantic facts their interaction actually earns.

Accessibility does not inherit World Lab's spatial ontology or implementation.

World Lab also preserves the open research question of forkable extensibility: whether divergent descendants can inherit and return semantic discoveries without requiring implementation merges.

## Digital Familiar

Source: https://github.com/bonoj/DigitalFamiliar

Digital Familiar is evidence for the organizational inheritance pattern used here.

It inherits a research practice from World Lab without forking or duplicating World Lab's implementation or research authority. It establishes its own Semantic Surface, research questions, and eventual executable evidence.

Accessibility follows the same pattern.

## Foundry

Source: https://github.com/bonoj/Foundry

Foundry provides useful experiential evidence that a sparse executable substrate can grow sophisticated behavior through concrete entities and relationships.

The important antecedent is not Foundry's specific ECS, terrain, payload vocabulary, or visual realization.

The useful observation is that construction can proceed by making small things concrete:

- something exists;
- it produces, consumes, moves, stores, routes, attacks, or transforms something;
- relationships become visible;
- composition exposes missing vocabulary;
- executable behavior makes the next question easier to ask.

Accessibility is interested in whether comparable expressive growth can occur while demanding substantially less interface precision and prerequisite knowledge from the person constructing it.

## Current human observation

The immediate motivating observation is practical rather than theoretical.

Existing construction interfaces can make physical interaction, visual acuity, technical vocabulary, or procedural knowledge consume attention that the builder would rather spend on the thing being built.

Model-mediated construction may be able to absorb some of that interface burden.

One concrete requirement already exposed is extreme text enlargement: important textual interaction may sometimes need to occupy most of a phone screen and must remain usable rather than becoming a broken scaled-up desktop interface.

Another is that voice, while potentially important, is only one accessibility mechanism. The project should not collapse accessibility into speech input.

## What has not yet been demonstrated

This prior evidence does not demonstrate that:

- an almost-empty accessible construction substrate will work;
- Foundry-level expressive power can be preserved with dramatically lower interaction demands;
- a model can reliably infer incomplete construction intent;
- extreme text enlargement can coexist gracefully with a spatial runtime;
- multiple reference modalities can share semantic identity without confusion;
- accessibility discoveries will transfer cleanly back to other project lineages.

Those are available research questions, not inherited conclusions.
